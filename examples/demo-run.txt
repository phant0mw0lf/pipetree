What this demonstrates, table by table:

- bronze.customer fails outright on its one attempt (a non-transient
  ValueError - "malformed export file" isn't the kind of error a retry
  fixes) and is never retried.
- Its two dependents in the tree, silver.customer_enriched and
  gold.dim_customer, are marked upstream_failed the instant bronze.customer
  fails - they never start. gold.fact_sales is also upstream_failed: it
  depends on gold.dim_customer, so the failure propagates transitively
  even though fact_sales's *other* dependency (silver.orders) succeeds.
- bronze.orders is throttled (a transient 429) twice, then succeeds on its
  third attempt: retried→succeeded, not just succeeded, so the digest
  distinguishes "worked first try" from "needed the retry logic."
- bronze.employee has no dependents and no injected fault - it starts
  immediately, runs independently of the customer/orders failure and
  retries happening elsewhere, and finishes as an ordinary succeeded row.
  The run's failure doesn't touch it at all.
- silver.orders starts the moment bronze.orders finishes (not when its
  *layer* finishes) and succeeds - proof the scheduler walks the
  dependency tree, not bronze/silver/gold as execution barriers.

Exit code 1: the run as a whole is not a success (one table failed, three
were never run), even though four of the seven tables completed.
