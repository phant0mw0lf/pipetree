Columns are waves: a table sits in wave k when its longest chain of dependencies is k tables long, so a column's tables can run in parallel (up to max_workers at a time). The executor doesn't wait for a whole wave - a table starts the moment its own parents have succeeded. Hover a table for its edges and details.
